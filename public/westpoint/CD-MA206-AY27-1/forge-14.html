Interpret each predictor:

  • weight: holding all else constant, a one pound increase in pack weight is a 1.28 minute increase in ruck time, on average.
  • run: holding all else constant, a one minute increase in 2 mile run time is a 3.67 minute increase in ruck time, on average.
  • height: holding all else constant, a one inch increase in height is a 1.27 minute decrease in ruck time, on average.
  • gpa: holding all else constant, a one point increase in GPA is a 0.67 minute decrease in ruck time, on average. Not significant, so no evidence of a real effect.
  • routeRoad: holding all else constant, a Road ruck is 17.81 minutes faster than a Mountain ruck, on average.
  • routeTrail: holding all else constant, a Trail ruck is 9.23 minutes faster than a Mountain ruck, on average.

\(R^2\) = 94.1%: the model explains 94.1% of the variation in ruck time.

ANOVA

Why Not Just Run t Tests?

AFT scores for 25 cadets in each of four companies. Do the company means differ?

Four companies means six two-sample t tests:

Alpha vs Bravo Alpha vs Charlie Alpha vs Delta
Bravo vs Charlie Bravo vs Delta Charlie vs Delta

Each test has a 5% chance of a false positive. Run six and the chance of at least one is about \(1 - 0.95^6 = 0.26\). ANOVA asks the question once.

The F Test

\(H_0: \mu_{\text{Alpha}} = \mu_{\text{Bravo}} = \mu_{\text{Charlie}} = \mu_{\text{Delta}}\)

\(H_a:\) at least two of the means differ

How the Table Is Calculated

\(I\) = 4 companies, \(J\) = 25 cadets each. \(\bar{x}_{i\cdot}\) is company \(i\)’s mean, \(\bar{x}_{\cdot\cdot}\) the overall mean.

Source df Sum of Squares Mean Square \(F\)
Treatments \(I - 1\) \(\text{SSTr} = J \sum_i (\bar{x}_{i\cdot} - \bar{x}_{\cdot\cdot})^2\) \(\text{MSTr} = \dfrac{\text{SSTr}}{I - 1}\) \(\dfrac{\text{MSTr}}{\text{MSE}}\)
Error \(I(J - 1)\) \(\text{SSE} = \sum_i \sum_j (x_{ij} - \bar{x}_{i\cdot})^2\) \(\text{MSE} = \dfrac{\text{SSE}}{I(J - 1)}\)
Total \(IJ - 1\) \(\text{SST} = \text{SSTr} + \text{SSE}\)

With the AFT data:

Source df Sum of Squares Mean Square \(F\)
Treatments 3 18,054 18,054 / 3 = 6,018 6,018 / 2,058 = 2.92
Error 96 197,585 197,585 / 96 = 2,058
Total 99 215,639

P-value: 1 - pf(2.92, 3, 96) = 0.038.

Interpretation

  • Decision: P-value = 0.038 < 0.05, so reject \(H_0\).
  • Conclusion: there is convincing evidence that at least two companies have different mean AFT scores.
  • What we cannot say: which companies differ. That is Tukey, Lesson 36.
